赛博肾脏植入。迎接机械飞升！

选择由血肉改进而来的相对便宜的赛博强化肾脏（半机械），或双倍强劲的赛博仿生肾脏。

### **赛博肾脏的优点：**

- 依靠科技的力量，一个肾就够！
- 对[失血 Blood Loss](/afflictions/blood-loss) 与[出血 Bleeding](/afflictions/bleeding) 的抗性
- 将血型改变成C+，能输各种血而不用担心有[输血性休克 Hemotransfusion Shock](/afflictions/hemotransfusion-shock)
- 对[肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage) 的抗性

### **赛博肾脏的缺点：**

- 将血型改变成C+，不能给除了C+血型外的任何人输血，否则会导致[输血性休克 Hemotransfusion Shock](/afflictions/hemotransfusion-shock)
- 定期经历赛博精神病（常规精神病），这可以通过以任何方式降低[免疫力](/afflictions/immunity)来避免，例如非常实惠的免疫抑制剂吸入器。

## **如何添加：**

进行[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) ，使用赛博肾脏替换苦弱的血肉肾脏。收着腰子记得存起来留着将来升级。

**机械工程技能需求：60**

医疗/外科技能需求：70

**应用成功时：**

安装植入物，治疗肾脏器官损伤和部分[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage)

**应用失败时（机械不达标）：**

安装植入物但造成20%[肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage)

**应用失败时（医疗不达标）：**

安装植入物但造成[内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding)

## **如何移除：**

[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery)  会切除并返还安装的赛博肾脏。

A cybernetic kidney replacement. Embrace the certainty of steel!

Available as a more affordable Cyber-augmented Kidney (half meat, half silicon) crafted from an extracted Kidney, or the twice as effective fully-synthetic Cyberkidney.

### Advantages of Cyberkidneys:

- With the power of techology, you only need one!
- Resistance to [失血 Blood Loss](/afflictions/blood-loss)  and [出血 Bleeding](/afflictions/bleeding)
- Switches blood type to C+, which can accept any other blood without [输血性休克 Hemotransfusion Shock](/afflictions/hemotransfusion-shock)
- Resistance to [肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage)

#### Disadvantages of Cyberorgans:

- Switches blood type to C+, whose cybernanites will cause [输血性休克 Hemotransfusion Shock](/afflictions/hemotransfusion-shock) if given to anyone else lacking cybernetic blood.
- Can periodically experience fits of Cyberpsychosis (regular psychosis), which can be avoided by reducing your Immunity in any way, such as the very affordable Immunosuppressant Inhaler.

## How to add:

Do [器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery), using the Cyberkidney instead of a pair of feeble meat kidneys. Consider refrigerating the procured meat for upgrading into a Cyber-augmented Kidney.

#### Mechanical skill requirement: 60

Medical/Surgical skill requirement: 70

#### Application Success:

Installs the cyberorgan, healing that organ’s damage and some [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage) .

#### Application Failure (Mechanical):

Same as Success but causes 20% [肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage)    .

#### Application Failure (Medical):

Same as Success but causes [内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding).

## How to remove:

[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) will remove and return the corresponding cybernetic organ.
