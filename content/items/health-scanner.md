一个对诊断极为重要的物品，使用电池供电。使用时，你将得到对“健康扫描仪可见”的所有苦痛的读数，被扫描的病人将每秒获得0.5%的[辐射病](/afflictions/radiation-sickness)，而你（扫描的人）将每秒获得0.3%辐射病。此外，人们可以通过在健康界面中使用健康扫描仪来扫描自己，就像使用绷带一样。这样则会造成1%的辐射病，还可以扫描个别肢体以确认[异物](/afflictions/foreign-bodies)的部位。
An item which is extremely important for diagnosis. Upon use and charged with batteries, you will get the readout of all afflictions visible to the health scanner, and the patient you're scanning will gain 0.5% [辐射病 Radiation Sickness](/afflictions/radiation-sickness) per second, and you (the person scanning) will gain 0.3% per second. Additionally, one can scan themselves by using the health scanner in the interface like a treatment, as you would a bandage. This gives 1% radiation sickness, and also allows you to scan individual limbs to find things such as [体内异物 Foreign Bodies](/afflictions/foreign-bodies).
