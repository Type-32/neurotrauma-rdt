发热是一种症状，这意味着它是非致命的，不会引起任何其他的苦痛。
Fever is a symptom, which means that it isn't fatal and doesn't cause any other afflictions.

---

\## 由以下原因造成：

- [败血症](/afflictions/sepsis) (5%及以上)
- [戒断](/afflictions/withdrawal) (酒精戒断，90%及以上)
- [麻醉 Anesthesia](/afflictions/anesthesia)
