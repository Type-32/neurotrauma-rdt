视野模糊是一种症状，这意味着它是非致命的，不会引起任何其他的苦痛。顾名思义，这会使你的视力变得模糊。
Blurred vision is a symptom, which means that it isn't fatal and doesn't cause any other afflictions.

---

\## 由以下原因造成：

- [低血压](/afflictions/hypotension) (55%及以下)
- [脑震荡](/afflictions/concussion)
- [麻醉 Anesthesia](/afflictions/anesthesia)
