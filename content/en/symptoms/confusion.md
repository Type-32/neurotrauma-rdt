混乱是一种症状，这意味着它是非致命的，不会引起任何其他的苦痛。
Confusion is a symptom, which means that it isn't fatal and doesn't cause any other afflictions.

---

\## 由以下原因造成：

- [酸中毒](/afflictions/acidosis) (15%及以上)
- [败血症](/afflictions/sepsis) (40%及以上)
- [低血压](/afflictions/hypotension) (30%及以下)
- [低血氧](/afflictions/hypoxemia) (50%及以上)
- [戒断](/afflictions/withdrawal) (酒精戒断，80%及以上)
- [脑震荡](/afflictions/concussion)
- [麻醉 Anesthesia](/afflictions/anesthesia)
