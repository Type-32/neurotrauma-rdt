呕吐通常是无害的，有时可能是有益的，因为它会导致碱中毒。呕吐也会使你的角色速度减少20%。
Vomiting usually isn't harmful, and can sometimes be beneficial due to it causing alkalosis. Vomiting will also slow your character down by 20%.

---
##由以下原因造成：

- [输血性休克](/afflictions/hemotransfusion-shock) (在0-40%之间)
- [戒断](/afflictions/withdrawal)(酒精戒断，60%及以上)
- 酒精中毒(达到100%)
- [肾脏损伤](/afflictions/kidney-damage) (60%及以上)
- [迅猛龙杀手精华](/items/raptor-bane-extract)

- [碱中毒](/afflictions/alkalosis)
