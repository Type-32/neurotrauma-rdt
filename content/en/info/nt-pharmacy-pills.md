制药拓展（NT Pharmacy）可以把药物压成药丸：更小的剂量、更高的产量，或更便宜的原料。下面是[医疗箱建议](/info/yiliaoxiangjianyi)和指南里提到的几种药丸。配方顺序：基料、黏合剂、填料、有效成分。

The NT Pharmacy expansion presses medicine into pills: smaller doses, more of them, or cheaper ingredients. These are the pills the [medkit loadouts](/info/yiliaoxiangjianyi) and other cards mention. Recipes list base, binder, filler and active ingredient.

另见 / See also: [制药拓展指南 NT Pharmacy Guide](/info/nt-pharmacy-guide)

## 速效救心丸 Cardiac arrest / heart attack pills

治愈100%的[心搏骤停](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest)和50%的[心脏病发作](/afflictions/heart-attack)。产量2个药丸。

Cures 100% [Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest) and 50% [Heart Attack](/afflictions/heart-attack). Yields 2 pills.

- 基料 Base: [异星血浆 Alien Blood](/items/alien-blood) 或 or [血袋 Blood Packs](/items/blood-packs)
- 黏合剂 Binder: 乙醇 Ethanol
- 填料 Filler: 无 none
- 有效成分 Active: [硝化甘油 Nitroglycerin](/items/nitroglycerin)

## 无痛药丸 Dislocation pills

最轻微的[镇痛](/afflictions/analgesia)效果，刚好够把脱臼的四肢掰回去。

The most minor [Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia), just enough to set dislocated limbs back in place more easily.

- 基料 Base: 随便（别用盐水和林格氏液）/ whatever is handy (not saline or Ringer's solution)
- 黏合剂 Binder: 乙醇 Ethanol
- 填料 Filler: 2× 硅 Silicon
- 有效成分 Active: [药用鸦片 Opium](/items/opium)

## 感染药丸 Minor husk / sepsis pills

12.5%的[广谱抗生素](/items/broad-spectrum-antibiotics)效果（原药为50%）。再加一个硅和广谱抗生素可省下乙醇和血、产量翻倍；加一个钠可略微提高效力。

12.5% of a [Broad-spectrum Antibiotics](/items/broad-spectrum-antibiotics) dose (a full dose is 50%). Add another silicon and antibiotics to save ethanol and blood and double the yield; add sodium for a little more potency.

- 基料 Base: [血袋 Blood Packs](/items/blood-packs) 或 or [异星血浆 Alien Blood](/items/alien-blood)
- 黏合剂 Binder: 乙醇 Ethanol
- 填料 Filler: 1× 硅 Silicon
- 有效成分 Active: [广谱抗生素 Broad-spectrum Antibiotics](/items/broad-spectrum-antibiotics)

## 小手术丸 Minor surgery pills

把3支[吗啡](/items/morphine)变成4粒，[镇痛](/afflictions/analgesia)时间不错，效力损失约10%。再加一个硅可让每粒效力减半、数量更多。

Stretches 3 [Morphine](/items/morphine) into 4 pills for decent [Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia), losing about 10% potency. Another silicon halves each pill's potency for more, smaller doses.

- 基料 Base: [异星血浆 Alien Blood](/items/alien-blood)
- 黏合剂 Binder: 乙醇 Ethanol
- 填料 Filler: 1× 硅 Silicon
- 有效成分 Active: 3× [吗啡 Morphine](/items/morphine)

## 神经伤药丸 Neurotrauma pills

低剂量（20%–34%）的[甘露醇](/items/mannitol)效果。只有在有至少2个钠，或1个异星血浆加1个钠时才值得做，否则得到的甘露醇效力是负的。

A small (20%–34%) [Mannitol](/items/mannitol) effect. Only worth making with at least 2 sodium, or 1 alien blood and 1 sodium; otherwise you lose mannitol potency overall.

- 基料 Base: [异星血浆 Alien Blood](/items/alien-blood)
- 黏合剂 Binder: [甘露醇 Mannitol](/items/mannitol)
- 填料 Filler: 2× 钠 Sodium
- 有效成分 Active: [硫胺素 Thiamine](/items/thiamine)

## 驱虫药丸 Major husk pills

45%效力的画皮寄生虫治疗药。画皮感染在40%时才会显示，所以动作快的话一粒就能治好。3个皮虫净可做8粒（用甘露醇作黏合剂可做16粒）。

45% husk cure pills. Husk infection only shows at 40%, so if you're quick one pill cures it. 3 calyxanide make 8 pills (16 with mannitol as the binder).

- 基料 Base: [异星血浆 Alien Blood](/items/alien-blood)
- 黏合剂 Binder: 乙醇 Ethanol，或想要双倍药丸时用 or, for double the pills, [甘露醇 Mannitol](/items/mannitol)
- 填料 Filler: 2× 硅 Silicon
- 有效成分 Active: 3× 皮虫净 Calyxanide
