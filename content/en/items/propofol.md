**异丙酚不再可以制造**

一种用于使病人[昏迷](/afflictions/unconsciousness)以防止[创伤性休克](/afflictions/traumatic-shock)的麻醉药物。

使用后，患者将以每秒0.3%的速度获得[麻醉 Anesthesia](/afflictions/anesthesia) 效果。麻醉效果在1-15%之间时，患者将获得5%的[镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia) 效果。超过15%的麻醉效果后，患者将失去意识。麻醉效果达到100%时，患者将不再受到异丙酚的影响。使用[纳洛酮 Naloxone](/items/naloxone) 会使麻醉效果降至0%，并消除其作用。

可能的副作用包括：

- [低血压 Hypotension](/afflictions/hypotension)
- [混乱 Confusion](/symptoms/confusion)
- [视野模糊 Blurred Vision](/symptoms/blurred-vision)
- [发热 Fever](/symptoms/fever)
- [心颤 Fibrillation](/afflictions/fibrillation)
- [癫痫发作 Seizure](/afflictions/seizure)
- [吐血 Vomiting Blood](/symptoms/vomiting-blood)
- 精神病

**Propofol is no longer craftable**

A drug used to induce controlled [失去意识 Unconsciousness](/afflictions/unconsciousness)  for the purpose of preventing [创伤性休克 Traumatic Shock](/afflictions/traumatic-shock).

Upon use, the patient will gain [麻醉 Anesthesia](/afflictions/anesthesia) at 0.3% per second. Between 1-15% anesthesia, the patient will have 5% [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia). Beyond 15% anesthesia, the patient will be unconscious. At 100% anesthesia, the patient will no longer be under the effect of propofol. Application of [麻痹治愈剂 Anaparalyzant](/items/anaparalyzant) will reduce anesthesia to 0%, also removing its effect.

Side effects of propofol include:

- [低血压 Hypotension](/afflictions/hypotension)
- [混乱 Confusion](/symptoms/confusion)
- [视野模糊 Blurred Vision](/symptoms/blurred-vision)
- [发热 Fever](/symptoms/fever)
- [心颤 Fibrillation](/afflictions/fibrillation)
- [癫痫发作 Seizure](/afflictions/seizure)
- [吐血 Vomiting Blood](/symptoms/vomiting-blood)
- Psychosis
