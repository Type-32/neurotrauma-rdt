### **医疗水平要求：100**

一个重要的器官。患者的意识和神经创伤与之直接相关。与其他器官不同，大脑通常不会移植。

在头部的[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) 中，大脑可能会被切除或移植。切除大脑会导致立即[失去意识 Unconsciousness](/afflictions/unconsciousness) 、[心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest) 、[呼吸停止 Respiratory Arrest](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest) 并失去对角色的控制（患者将进入观察者状态）和100%的[神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma) ，以及持续快速的神经创伤增加，只要器官被切除，所有症状都会持续下去。器官切除出来的状况与先前所受的神经创伤成反比，最高是100%。在100%或更多的神经创伤下切除大脑不会产生大脑物品，患者会立即达到200%的神经创伤，导致死亡。



如果将大脑移植回患者体内，他们将重新控制角色，但他们的心搏骤停和呼吸停止仍必须接受治疗。如果将大脑移植到另一名患者体内，大脑原主将控制该患者。



与其他器官不同，即使在冷藏容器中，大脑的耐久也会迅速降低，除非将其放置在[脑罐 Brain Jar](/items/brain-jar) 中。

### Medical skill check: 100

A vital organ. The patient’s consciousness and [神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma) are directly linked to it. Unlike other organs, the brain is typically not transplanted.

The brain may be removed or transplanted during an [器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) done on the head. Removal of the brain causes immediate [失去意识 Unconsciousness](/afflictions/unconsciousness), [心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest), [呼吸停止 Respiratory Arrest](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest), loss of character control (the patient will enter freecam), and 100% [神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma), and continuous rapid neurotrauma gain, all of which will persist as long as the organ is removed.. The condition of the organ is inversely proportional to neurotrauma, up to 100%. Removing a brain at 100% or more neurotrauma will not produce a brain transplant and the patient will immediately reach 200% neurotrauma, killing them.

If the brain is transplanted back into the patient, they will regain control of the character, but their [心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest) and [呼吸停止 Respiratory Arrest](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest)  must still be treated. If the brain is transplanted into another patient, the donor will take control of that patient.

Unlike other organs, the condition of the brain will always decay rapidly, even in a refrigerated container, unless it is placed in a [脑罐 Brain Jar](/items/brain-jar). If the brain or the container it is in ever touches the ground, it will be destroyed, unless if it is in a brain jar.
