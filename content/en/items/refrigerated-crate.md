一种与防水板条箱[Crates](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Crates)  类似的容器。它有12个格子，可以容纳任何小物品。不过，它主要用来保存移植的器官（除了[大脑 Brain Transplant](/items/brain-transplant) ）、断肢和凝胶冰袋。能够防止物品自然损坏。移植器官损坏高于90%、四肢损坏高于95%时它们能够自然恢复。凝胶冰袋在任何条件下都能自行恢复。

此容器可以冷藏：

- [心脏 Heart Transplant](/items/heart-transplant)
- [肾脏 Kidney Transplant](/items/kidney-transplant)
- [肝脏 Liver Transplant](/items/liver-transplant)
- [肺 Lung Transplant](/items/lung-transplant)
- [手臂（仿生/断臂）Arm (Bionic / Severed)](/items/arm-bionic-severed)
- [腿（仿生/断肢） Leg (Bionic / Severed)](/items/leg-bionic-severed)

A container which shares many properties with waterproof [Crates](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Crates). It has 12 slots and may hold any small item. However, its main use is to hold organ transplants (except for the [大脑 Brain Transplant](/items/brain-transplant)), severed limbs, and [凝胶冰袋 Gel Ice Pack](/items/gel-ice-pack)s. This will prevent the items from naturally losing condition. Organ transplants will slowly regain condition if they are above 90%, limbs above 95%, and gel ice packs at any condition.

Organ transplants may be destroyed if they are placed in the crate and it is dropped from a large height.

The container will refrigerate:

- [心脏 Heart Transplant](/items/heart-transplant)
- [肾脏 Kidney Transplant](/items/kidney-transplant)
- [肝脏 Liver Transplant](/items/liver-transplant)
- [肺 Lung Transplant](/items/lung-transplant)
- [手臂（仿生/断臂）Arm (Bionic / Severed)](/items/arm-bionic-severed)
- [腿（仿生/断肢） Leg (Bionic / Severed)](/items/leg-bionic-severed)
- [凝胶冰袋 Gel Ice Pack](/items/gel-ice-pack)
