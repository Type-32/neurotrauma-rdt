手术创巾是一件服装，用于提高穿戴者的无菌性，从而减少手术感染的风险。在穿着时，还能将[创伤性休克](/afflictions/traumatic-shock)的强度降低50%。

\+100基础无菌性

穿着时-50%[创伤性休克 Traumatic Shock](/afflictions/traumatic-shock) 增长

---

Surgical Drapes are a clothing item used to increase the wearer's sterility and thus decreasing the risk of Surgical Infection. They also reduce the gain of Traumatic Shock by 50% while being worn.
