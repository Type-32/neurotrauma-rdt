裹尸袋是一种用于运送尸体的物品，比简单地拖动要快得多。它占用潜水服的槽位，如果把活人放进去的话会使其窒息。在躯干使用[扳手 Wrench](/items/wrench) 可以强制移除裹尸袋，只要其内的人没有[失去意识 Unconsciousness](/afflictions/unconsciousness) 。移除掉的裹尸袋将放入扳手使用者的物品栏中。

---


The Body Bag is an item used to transport bodies much more quickly than simply dragging them. It is placed into the Diving Suit slot and will suffocate a living human if they are placed into it. It can be forcibly removed by using the [扳手 Wrench](/items/wrench) as a treatment on the torso of a person wearing it, as long as the person is not [失去意识 Unconsciousness](/afflictions/unconsciousness). Doing so will place it in the inventory of the wrench user.
