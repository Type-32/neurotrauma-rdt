用于在病人[换气不足](/afflictions/hypoventilation)或[停止呼吸](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest)时提供氧气的物品。

将救护包与普通氧气瓶一起使用，可以治疗90%的[缺氧](/afflictions/oxygen-low)，并使用氧气瓶中2%的氧气。

使用装有液氧罐的救护包可以治疗90%的[缺氧](/afflictions/oxygen-low)，治疗[呼吸停止](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest)，并使用液氧罐中2%的液氧。

使用装有焊接燃料罐的救护包将导致110%的[缺氧](/afflictions/oxygen-low)，造成10%的[肺部损伤](/afflictions/lung-damage)，并使用罐内5%的焊接燃料。

使用带有燃素燃料罐的救护包会导致110%的[缺氧](/afflictions/oxygen-low)，造成20%的肺部损伤，造成10%的[烧伤](/afflictions/burns)，并使用罐内5%的燃素燃料。

使用装有喷漆的急救包会造成50%的[缺氧](/afflictions/oxygen-low)，造成3%的肺部损伤，造成5%的化学成瘾，治疗20%的化学戒断，并消耗罐中5%的喷漆。
An item used to provide supplementary oxygen if a patient isn't breathing enough or isn't breathing at all.

Using the ambubag with a regular oxygen tank in it will treat 90% oxygen low, and use 2% of the oxygen in the tank.

Using the ambubag with an oxygenite tank in it will treat 90% oxygen low, cure [呼吸停止 Respiratory Arrest](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest), and use 2% of the oxygen in the tank.

Using the ambubag with a welding fuel tank in it will cause 110% oxygen low, cause 10% [肺部损伤 Lung Damage](/afflictions/lung-damage), and use 5% of the welding fuel in the tank.

Using the ambubag with a incendium fuel tank in it will cause 110% oxygen low, cause 20% lung damage, cause 10% [烧伤 Burns](/afflictions/burns), and use 5% of the welding fuel in the tank.

Using the ambubag with paint in it will cause 50% oxygen low, cause 3% lung damage, cause 5% chemical addiction, treat 20% chemical withdrawal, and use 5% of the paint in the can.
