Raptor Bane Extract is used in the treatment of [酸中毒 Acidosis](/afflictions/acidosis) by inducing [呕吐 Vomiting](/symptoms/vomiting) in the patient to give them [碱中毒 Alkalosis](/afflictions/alkalosis).
