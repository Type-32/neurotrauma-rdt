赛博心脏植入。迎接机械飞升！

选择由血肉改进而来的相对便宜的赛博强化心脏（半机械），或双倍强劲的赛博仿生心脏。

### **赛博心脏的优点：**

- 提高近战攻击速度和移动速度
- 提高20%最大生命值
- 恢复正常血压的速度翻倍
- 对[心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage) 的抗性

### **赛博心脏的缺点：**

- 定期经历赛博精神病（常规精神病），这可以通过以任何方式降低[免疫力](/afflictions/immunity)来避免，例如非常实惠的免疫抑制剂吸入器。

## **如何添加：**

进行[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) ，使用赛博心脏替换苦弱的血肉心脏。收着心记得存起来留着将来升级。

**机械工程技能需求：60**

医疗/外科技能需求：70

**应用成功时：**

安装植入物，治疗心脏器官损伤和部分[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage)

**应用失败时（机械不达标）：**

安装植入物但造成20%[心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage)

**应用失败时（医疗不达标）：**

安装植入物但造成[内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding)

## **如何移除：**

[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery)  会切除并返还安装的赛博心脏。

A cybernetic heart replacement. Embrace the certainty of steel!

Available as a more affordable Cyber-augmented Heart (half meat, half silicon) crafted from an extracted Heart, or the twice as effective fully-synthetic Cyberheart.

### Advantages of Cyberhearts:

- Improves melee attack speed and movement speed.
- Increases max health by 20%.
- Doubles natural rate at which Blood Pressure returns to match Blood Amount.
- Resistant to [心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage)

#### Disadvantages of Cyberorgans:

- Can periodically experience fits of Cyberpsychosis (regular psychosis), which can be avoided by reducing your Immunity in any way, such as the very affordable Immunosuppressant Inhaler.

## How to add:

Do [器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery), using the Cyberheart instead of a feeble meat heart. Consider refridgerating the procured meat heart for upgrading into a Cyber-augmented Heart.

#### Mechanical skill requirement: 60

Medical/Surgical skill requirement: 70

#### Application Success:

Installs the cyberorgan, healing that organ’s damage and some [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage) .

#### Application Failure (Mechanical):

Same as Success but causes 20% [心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage) .

#### Application Failure (Medical):

Same as Success but causes [内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding).

## How to remove:

[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) will remove and return the corresponding cybernetic organ.
