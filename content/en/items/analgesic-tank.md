A tank of analgesics which can be used in the [手术台 Surgery Table](/items/surgery-table) or [急救呼吸气囊 Ambubag](/items/ambubag). It can be bought or crafted, and refilled.

When used in a Surgery Table, provides up to 14% [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia) to the patient laying on the table, and its condition reduces by 0.7% per second.

When used in an Ambubag, condition reduces by 25%, and provides:

- +56% [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia)
- +40%[Opiate Addiction](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Opiate_Addiction)
- +30% [阿片类药物中毒 Opiate Overdose](/afflictions/opiate-overdose)
- -75% [Opiate Withdrawal](/afflictions/withdrawal)

If the patient is more than 30% [酒精中毒 Drunk](/afflictions/drunk), instead provides:

- +106% [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia)
- +40%[Opiate Addiction](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Opiate_Addiction)
- +60% [阿片类药物中毒 Opiate Overdose](/afflictions/opiate-overdose)
- -75% [Opiate Withdrawal](/afflictions/withdrawal)
