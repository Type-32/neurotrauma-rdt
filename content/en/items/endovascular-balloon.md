## 只有在设置里启用“Hardmode Aortic Rupture”时才可用。

[主动脉破裂手术](/procedures/aortic-rupture-surgery)的第三步。如果没有100%的[手术切口](/afflictions/surgery-incision)，球囊就**无法**发挥作用。使用球囊可以治疗[内出血](/afflictions/internal-bleeding)并阻断主动脉血流，这将阻止由主动脉破裂引起的[失血](/afflictions/blood-loss)，但也会引起[坏疽](/afflictions/gangrene)。
The third step in [主动脉破裂手术 Aortic Rupture Surgery](/procedures/aortic-rupture-surgery). The balloon will **not** work without 100% [手术切口 Surgery Incision](/afflictions/surgery-incision). Using the balloon will cure [内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding) and block the aorta, which will prevent [失血 Blood Loss](/afflictions/blood-loss) caused by aortic rupture, but will also cause [坏疽 Gangrene](/afflictions/gangrene).
