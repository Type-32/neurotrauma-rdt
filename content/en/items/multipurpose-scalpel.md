多功能手术刀结合了普通手术刀、器官切除手术刀和创伤剪的功能，同时也为使用者提供了故意伤害患者的方法。

可以在加工台里制作它。

装备后点击UI内的选项进行模式更改。

### 模式: 手术切口

在此模式下起和[手术刀 Scalpel](/items/scalpel) 相同的作用。

### 模式: 绷带，石膏，不端行为

在此模式下，若患者身上带有石膏固定或绷带包扎，则起和[创伤剪 Trauma Shears](/items/trauma-shears) 相同的作用。

若无以上效果，则会刺伤患者，根据刺伤部位不同可能产生不同的严重后果。

可能造成以下后果：

- 对正在进行手术的四肢造成骨折。
- 对正在进行手术的头部造成神经创伤。
- 对正在进行手术的躯干造成特定器官的损伤和内出血。

### 模式: 自适应器官摘取

在此模式下，可以从患者体内切除特定器官，同时也是[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) 必不可少的一步。器官摘取对象取决于哪个躯干上有[皮肤牵开 Retracted Skin](/afflictions/retracted-skin) 与手术刀在哪个肢体使用。

皮肤牵开：躯干
左臂：[肾脏 Kidney Transplant](/items/kidney-transplant)
躯干：[肝脏 Liver Transplant](/items/liver-transplant)
右臂：[心脏 Heart Transplant](/items/heart-transplant)
左腿：[肺 Lung Transplant](/items/lung-transplant)

皮肤牵开：头部
头部：[大脑 Brain Transplant](/items/brain-transplant)

The Multipurpose Scalpel combines the functionality of the regular scalpel, the organ procurement scalpels, and the trauma shears, while also providing the user with a way of intentionally harming the patient.

This item is craftable at a regular fabricator.

The mode of this Scalpel is changed by equipping the item (akin to a weapon or tool) and clicking on the available UI buttons which appear.

### Mode: Surgery Incision

In this mode, the item behaves identically to a regular [手术刀 Scalpel](/items/scalpel).

### Mode: Bandages, Casts, Malpractice

In this mode, as long as the patient has a bandage or a cast, the item functions identically to [创伤剪 Trauma Shears](/items/trauma-shears).
Is this not the case, then using it will result in the patient getting stabbed. Depending on location and surgical status of the patient, this can cause serious damage.

Notable outcomes include:

- a chance for causing fractures in extremeties that are currently opened for surgery
- causing neurotrauma on opened heads
- causing specific organ damage and internal bleeding on opened torsos

### Mode: Adaptive Organ Extraction

In this mode, the item will have the ability to extract any organ from a patient, and it is a required item for [器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery). The organ extracted depends on which limb has [皮肤牵开 Retracted Skin](/afflictions/retracted-skin), and which limb the Multipurpose Scalpel is used on:

Retracted skin: Torso
Left arm: [肾脏 Kidney Transplant](/items/kidney-transplant)
Torso: [肝脏 Liver Transplant](/items/liver-transplant)
Right arm: [心脏 Heart Transplant](/items/heart-transplant)
Left leg: [肺 Lung Transplant](/items/lung-transplant)

Retracted skin: Head
Head: [大脑 Brain Transplant](/items/brain-transplant)
