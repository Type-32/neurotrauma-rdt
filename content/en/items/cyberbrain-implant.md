赛博大脑植入。迎接机械飞升！

选择相对便宜的赛博强化大脑植入物，和双倍强劲的赛博尖端大脑植入物。

### **赛博大脑植入物的优点：**

- 全技能加5（尖端植入物加10）
- 永久轻度[镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia) 以便进行小手术
- 提高[神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma) 恢复速度
  - [甘露醇 Mannitol](/items/mannitol) 双倍效果
  - 双倍的自然恢复速度
- 对[神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma)  的轻微抗性

### **赛博器官的缺点:**

- 定期经历赛博精神病（常规精神病），这可以通过以任何方式降低免疫力来避免，例如非常实惠的免疫抑制剂吸入器。

## **如何添加：**

在头部进行标准手术流程到[皮肤牵开 Retracted Skin](/afflictions/retracted-skin) ，但不要移除脑子！留着还有用呢！对切口使用植入物。

**电气工程技能需求：60**

医疗/外科技能需求：80

**应用成功时：**

安装植入物，治疗部分[神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma) 与[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage)

**应用失败时（电气不达标）：**

植入成功但造成20%[神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma)

**应用失败时（医疗不达标）：**

植入成功但造成[内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding)

## **如何移除：**

[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) 中使用**一次**[https://trello.com/c/jqWPTZPq](https://trello.com/c/jqWPTZPq) 会切下植入物，使用**两次**会切除大脑，所以做手术时带着点脑子。

A cybernetic brain booster. Embrace the certainty of steel!

Available as a more affordable Cyber-augmented Brain Implant, or the twice as effective top-of-the-line ‘Cyberbrain’ Positronic Implant.

### Advantages of Cyberbrain Implants:

- Boosts all skills by 5 (10 for Positronic)
- Grants permanent mild [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia)  for easier surgeries
- Improves [神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma)  recovery rate
  - Doubles effects of [甘露醇 Mannitol](/items/mannitol)
  - Doubles natural healing rate once causes are addressed
- Mild resistance to [神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma)

#### Disadvantages of Cyberorgans:

- Can periodically experience fits of Cyberpsychosis (regular psychosis), which can be avoided by reducing your Immunity in any way, such as the very affordable Immunosuppressant Inhaler.

## How to add:

Open up the head as a typical surgery with[皮肤牵开 Retracted Skin](/afflictions/retracted-skin), but do not remove their brain! They’ll still need to keep that! Instead, place the Implant on top.

#### Electrical skill requirement: 60

Medical/Surgical skill requirement: 80

#### Application Success:

Installs the implant, healing some [神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma)  and some [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage) .

#### Application Failure (Mechanical):

Same as Success but causes 20% [神经创伤 Neurotrauma](/afflictions/neurotrauma).

#### Application Failure (Medical):

Same as Success but causes [内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding).

## How to remove:

[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) _once_ will return the Implant, using the [https://trello.com/c/pYZLWrbE/148-organ-procurement-scalpels](https://trello.com/c/pYZLWrbE/148-organ-procurement-scalpels) a second time will remove the patient’s brain, which they probably need.
