#### 仅在启用'Item Availability'时可用。

消毒剂是一种物品，其唯一用途是装入[消毒喷雾器](/items/antiseptic-sprayer)，每喷一次将消耗10%消毒剂。
Antiseptic is an item whose only use is to be loaded into the [消毒喷雾器 Antiseptic Sprayer](/items/antiseptic-sprayer). For each spray of the antiseptic sprayer, 10% condition will be used from the antiseptic.
