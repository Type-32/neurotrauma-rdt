### **医疗水平要求：40**

一个重要的器官。与患者的[肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage) 有直接关系。与其他器官不同，人都有两个肾，其损伤程度分开计算，当受到50%的[肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage)  时，一个肾将失去功能，另一个则依然工作。只有一个肾的患者也能保持健康，50%的肾脏损伤不会导致致命的[高血压 Hypertension](/afflictions/hypertension) 。

肾脏可以在躯干的[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) 中获得。摘除肾脏会一次摘除两个，造成100%的肾脏损伤以及最高20%的[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage)  ，肾的状态与受到的肾脏损伤成反比。因此，在25%肾损伤时切除肾脏将产生一个100%的肾脏和另一个50%的肾脏。

将**一个**肾脏移植回患者体内时，患者的最高50%[肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage)   与最高10%的[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage)  将根据肾脏的状态被相对治愈。如果患者的[免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) 高于10%，移植后可能出现[器官排斥 Organ Rejection](/afflictions/organ-rejection) ，前提是该机制打开。

如果不放在[手提器官冷藏箱 Refrigerated Container](/items/refrigerated-container) 或[器官冷藏集装箱 Refrigerated Crate](/items/refrigerated-crate) 中，肾脏将慢慢腐烂。肾脏的状态高于90%时会在冷藏时慢慢恢复，如果器官或其所在的容器从高处撞击地面，它会直接撞烂掉。

### Medical skill check: 40

A pair of vital organs. The patient’s [肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage) is directly related to it. Unlike other organs, patients will have two kidneys, which are individually damaged. At 50% kidney damage, only one kidney has failed, while the other kidney will be functional. A patient may be healthy with only one kidney. By itself, 50% kidney damage will not cause lethal [高血压 Hypertension](/afflictions/hypertension).

The kidneys may be removed or transplanted during an [器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) done on the torso. Removing the kidneys will remove both at once, causing 100%[肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage) and up to 20% [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage), inversely proportional to kidney damage before removal. The condition of the organs are inversely proportional to kidney damage before removal, up to 50% for each kidney. Therefore, removal of the kidneys at 25% kidney damage will produce one kidney at 100% and another one at 50%.

If the kidneys are transplanted back into the patient, up to 50% [肾脏损伤 Kidney Damage](/afflictions/kidney-damage) and up to 10% [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage) per kidney, both inversely proportional to their condition, will be treated. The patient may gain [器官排斥 Organ Rejection](/afflictions/organ-rejection) if they receive the organ and their [免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) is higher than 10%, if the mechanic is enabled in the mod config.

The condition of a kidney will decay unless placed in a [手提器官冷藏箱 Refrigerated Container](/items/refrigerated-container) or [器官冷藏集装箱 Refrigerated Crate](/items/refrigerated-crate). While refrigerated, the organ will slowly regain condition if it is above 90%, otherwise it will not change. If the organ or the container it is in is dropped from a large height, it will be destroyed.
