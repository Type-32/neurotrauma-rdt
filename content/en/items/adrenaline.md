肾上腺素主要用于[心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest) 的治疗，但也有多种功效。效果持续55s且没有技能需求。

肾上腺素的所有效果：

- +8% [Adrenaline Rush](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Adrenaline_Rush)（肾上腺素狂飙）（不会在血量低于0时保持患者清醒）
- +30%血压
- [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia) ，最大4.5%
- [心率加快 Increased Heartrate](/symptoms/increased-heartrate)
- [高血压 Hypertension](/afflictions/hypertension)
- +20%近战伤害
- [心颤 Fibrillation](/afflictions/fibrillation) 的增长速度减半
- 将减速效果的效用减半（例如[石膏固定 Plaster Cast](/afflictions/plaster-cast) 导致的减速）
- [脱臼 Dislocations](/afflictions/dislocations) 时可以强制行动，不会减速
- [骨折 Fractures](/afflictions/fractures) 时可以强制行动，不会减速。如果角色用骨折的手臂使用武器，会导致出血。如果角色腿部骨折，随着时间的推移会导致出血。

---

Adrenaline is mainly used in the treatment of [心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest), but has numerous other effects. Each adrenaline lasts 55 seconds and does not have a skill check.

All effects of adrenaline:

- +8% [Adrenaline Rush](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Adrenaline_Rush)(does not keep patient conscious below 0 vitality)
- +30% blood pressure
- [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia), up to 4.5%
- [心率加快 Increased Heartrate](/symptoms/increased-heartrate)
- [换气过度 Hyperventilation](/afflictions/hyperventilation)
- +20% melee damage
- Halves [心颤 Fibrillation](/afflictions/fibrillation) gain speed
- Halves the potency of slowing effects (such as from [石膏固定 Plaster Cast](/afflictions/plaster-cast))
- Prevents [脱臼 Dislocations](/afflictions/dislocations) from locking hands or slowing the character
- Prevents [骨折 Fractures](/afflictions/fractures) from locking hands or slowing the character. Causes bleeding if the character uses weapons held in the respective fractured arms. Causes bleeding over time if the character has fractured legs.
