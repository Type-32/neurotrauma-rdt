## 只有在设置里启用“Hardmode Aortic Rupture”时才可用。

[主动脉破裂手术](/procedures/aortic-rupture-surgery)的第四步。如果你事先没有使用[血管内球囊](/items/endovascular-balloon)，支架就不会起作用。使用支架可以治愈主动脉破裂，也会将血管内球囊从病人体内取出。
The fourth step in [主动脉破裂手术 Aortic Rupture Surgery](/procedures/aortic-rupture-surgery). The stent will **not** work if you have not used the [血管内球囊 Endovascular Balloon](/items/endovascular-balloon) beforehand. Using the medical stent will cure aortic rupture, and also remove the endovascular balloon from the patient.
