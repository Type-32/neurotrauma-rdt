### **医疗水平要求：40**

一个重要的器官。[肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage) 与其有直接关系。

肝脏可以在躯干的[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) 中获得。肝脏摘除会造成100%的[肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage)  以及最高20%的[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage) ，肝脏的状态与移除前的肝脏损伤成反比。

将肝脏移植回患者体内时，患者的[肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage)  与最高20%的[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage)  将根据肝脏的状态被相对治愈。如果患者的[免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) 高于10%，移植后可能出现[器官排斥 Organ Rejection](/afflictions/organ-rejection) ，前提是该机制打开。

如果不放在[手提器官冷藏箱 Refrigerated Container](/items/refrigerated-container) 或[器官冷藏集装箱 Refrigerated Crate](/items/refrigerated-crate) 中，肝脏将慢慢腐烂。肝脏的状态高于90%时会在冷藏时慢慢恢复，如果器官或其所在的容器从高处撞击地面，它会直接撞烂掉。

### Medical skill check: 40

A vital organ. The patient’s [肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage) is directly linked to it.

The liver may be removed or transplanted during an [器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) done on the torso. Removal of the liver causes 100% [肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage) and up to 20% [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage), inversely proportional to liver damage before removal. The condition of the organ is inversely proportional to liver damage before removal.

If a heart is transplanted back into the patient, their [肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage) and up to 20% [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage), both inversely proportional to the condition of the organ, will be treated. The patient may gain [器官排斥 Organ Rejection](/afflictions/organ-rejection) if they receive the organ and their [免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) is higher than 10%, if the mechanic is toggled on in the mod config.

The condition of a liver will decay unless placed in a [手提器官冷藏箱 Refrigerated Container](/items/refrigerated-container) or [器官冷藏集装箱 Refrigerated Crate](/items/refrigerated-crate). While refrigerated, the organ will slowly regain condition if it is above 90%, otherwise it will not change. If the organ or the container it is in hits the ground from a large height, it will be destroyed.
