### **医疗水平要求：40**

一个重要的器官。患者的心脏相关疾病，如[心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage) 与其有直接关系。



心脏可以在躯干的[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) 中获得。心脏摘除会立即导致[心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest) ，100%的[心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage) 以及最高20%的[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage) ，心脏的状态与移除前的心脏损伤成反比。患者的心脏被摘除时，[心脏压塞 Cardiac Tamponade](/afflictions/cardiac-tamponade) 与[心脏病发作 Heart Attack](/afflictions/heart-attack) 将无法出现，已有的病症会立刻移除。

将心脏移植回患者体内时，患者的[心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage)  与最高20%的[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage)  将根据心脏的状态被相对治愈，但[心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest)  不会。如果患者的[免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) 高于10%，移植后可能出现[器官排斥 Organ Rejection](/afflictions/organ-rejection) ，前提是该机制打开。

如果不放在[手提器官冷藏箱 Refrigerated Container](/items/refrigerated-container) 或[器官冷藏集装箱 Refrigerated Crate](/items/refrigerated-crate) 中，心脏将慢慢腐烂。心脏的状态高于90%时会在冷藏时慢慢恢复，如果器官或其所在的容器从高处撞击地面，它会直接撞烂掉。

### Medical skill check: 40

A vital organ. The patient’s heart-related afflictions, such as [心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage), are directly linked to it.

The heart may be removed or transplanted during an [器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) done on the torso. Removal of the heart causes immediate [心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest), 100% [心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage), and up to 20% [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage), inversely proportional to heart damage before removal. The condition of the organ is inversely proportional to heart damage before removal. The patient cannot gain and will be immediately relieved of [心脏压塞 Cardiac Tamponade](/afflictions/cardiac-tamponade) and [心脏病发作 Heart Attack](/afflictions/heart-attack) while their heart is removed.

If a heart is transplanted back into the patient, their [心脏损伤 Heart Damage](/afflictions/heart-damage) and up to 20% [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage), both inversely proportional to the condition of the organ, will be treated, but their [心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest) will not be treated. The patient may gain [器官排斥 Organ Rejection](/afflictions/organ-rejection) if they receive the organ and their [免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) is higher than 10%, if the mechanic is toggled on in the mod config.

The condition of a heart will decay unless placed in a [手提器官冷藏箱 Refrigerated Container](/items/refrigerated-container) or [器官冷藏集装箱 Refrigerated Crate](/items/refrigerated-crate). While refrigerated, the organ will slowly regain condition if it is above 90%, otherwise it will not change. If the organ or the container it is in hits the ground from a large height, it will be destroyed.
