赛博肝脏植入。迎接机械飞升！

选择由血肉改进而来的相对便宜的赛博强化肝脏（半机械），或双倍强劲的赛博仿生肝脏。

### **赛博肝脏的优点：**

- 对各种毒剂与[恶心 Nausea](/symptoms/nausea) 的抗性
- 对[肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage) 的抗性

### **赛博肝脏的缺点：**

- 定期经历赛博精神病（常规精神病），这可以通过以任何方式降低[免疫力](/afflictions/immunity)来避免，例如非常实惠的免疫抑制剂吸入器。

## **如何添加：**

进行[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) ，使用赛博肝脏替换苦弱的血肉肝脏。肝记得存起来留着将来升级。

**机械工程技能需求：60**

医疗/外科技能需求：70

**应用成功时：**

安装植入物，治疗肝脏器官损伤和部分[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage)

**应用失败时（机械不达标）：**

安装植入物但造成20%[肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage)

**应用失败时（医疗不达标）：**

安装植入物但造成[内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding)

## **如何移除：**

[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery)  会切除并返还安装的赛博肝脏。

A cybernetic liver replacement. Embrace the certainty of steel!

Available as a more affordable Cyber-augmented Liver (half meat, half silicon) crafted from an extracted Liver, or the twice as effective fully-synthetic Cyberliver.

### Advantages of Cyberlivers:

- Resistant to poisons, toxins, and [恶心 Nausea](/symptoms/nausea)
- Resistant to [肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage)

#### Disadvantages of Cyberorgans:

- Can periodically experience fits of Cyberpsychosis (regular psychosis), which can be avoided by reducing your Immunity in any way, such as the very affordable Immunosuppressant Inhaler.

## How to add:

Do [器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery), using the Cyberliver instead of a feeble meat liver. Consider refrigerating the procured meat for upgrading into a Cyber-augmented Liver.

#### Mechanical skill requirement: 60

Medical/Surgical skill requirement: 70

#### Application Success:

Installs the cyberorgan, healing that organ’s damage and some [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage) .

#### Application Failure (Mechanical):

Same as Success but causes 20% [肝脏损伤 Liver Damage](/afflictions/liver-damage)   .

#### Application Failure (Medical):

Same as Success but causes [内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding).

## How to remove:

[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) will remove and return the corresponding cybernetic organ.
