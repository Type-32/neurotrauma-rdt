用于替换左臂或右臂，可以连接到对应的手术截肢位置上。

被切断的手臂需要冷藏，否则会慢慢损耗掉。
仿生手臂是合成的，不需要冷藏，但在生物学上与原来的手臂相同。

## **用于治疗：**

- [坏疽 Gangrene](/afflictions/gangrene)
- 截肢
  - [创伤性截肢 Traumatic Amputation](/afflictions/traumatic-amputation)
  - [手术性截肢 Surgical Amputation](/afflictions/surgical-amputation)

## **如何添加：**

1. 对现有肢体进行完整的截肢手术，包括使用缝合线闭合，以切除任何现有肢体。
2. 重新进行截肢手术，直至进行到使用骨锯，以准备进行移植。
3. 缝合前对切口使用你要替换的那只手臂，然后缝合完成手术。

---

A replacement Left or Right arm that can be attached to a matching surgically amputated and prepared arm socket.

Severed arms require refrigeration or they will expire.
Bionic arms are synthetic and don’t require refrigeration, but are otherwise biologically equivalent to natural arms.

## Treats:

- [坏疽 Gangrene](/afflictions/gangrene)
- Missing arm
  - [创伤性截肢 Traumatic Amputation](/afflictions/traumatic-amputation)
  - [手术性截肢 Surgical Amputation](/afflictions/surgical-amputation)

## How to add:

1. Complete [Amputation Surgery](/procedures/amputation-surgery) on an existing limb, including a suture to close, to remove any existing limb.
2. Redo [Amputation Surgery](/procedures/amputation-surgery) up to having the limb bonesaw’d again to prepare the arm socket
3. Before suturing, use the replacement arm, and then suture closed to complete.
