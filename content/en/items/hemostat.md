大多数外科手术的第三步。如果没有100%的[手术切口](/afflictions/surgery-incision)，止血钳就**不会**起作用。使用它不需要医疗技能。成功地使用止血钳将使病人在使用该工具的肢体上获得[钳夹止血](/afflictions/clamped-bleeding)。
The third step in most surgical procedures. The hemostat will **not** work without 100% [手术切口 Surgery Incision](/afflictions/surgery-incision). Using it requires no medical skill. Successfully using the hemostat will give the patient [夹闭止血 Clamped Bleeding](/afflictions/clamped-bleeding) on the limb the tool was used on.
