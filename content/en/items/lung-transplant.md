### **医疗水平要求：40**

一个重要的器官。[肺部损伤 Lung Damage](/afflictions/lung-damage) 与其有直接关系。

肺可以在躯干的[器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) 中获得。肺摘除会造成100%的[肺部损伤 Lung Damage](/afflictions/lung-damage)  、[呼吸停止 Respiratory Arrest](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest) 以及最高20%的[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage) ，肺的状态与移除前的肺损伤成反比。患者的肺被摘除时，[气胸 Pneumothorax](/afflictions/pneumothorax) 将无法出现且已有的症状将被立刻移除。即使患者在肺部被切除的同时接受[人工通气](/items/surgery-table)或佩戴[自动心肺复苏器 Autopulse](/items/autopulse) ，患者也会获得[低血氧 Hypoxemia](/afflictions/hypoxemia) 。

将肺移植回患者体内时，患者的[肺部损伤 Lung Damage](/afflictions/lung-damage)   与最高20%的[原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage)  将根据肺的状态被相对治愈，但[呼吸停止 Respiratory Arrest](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest)  不会被治愈。如果患者的[免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) 高于10%，移植后可能出现[器官排斥 Organ Rejection](/afflictions/organ-rejection) ，前提是该机制打开。

如果不放在[手提器官冷藏箱 Refrigerated Container](/items/refrigerated-container) 或[器官冷藏集装箱 Refrigerated Crate](/items/refrigerated-crate) 中，肺将慢慢腐烂。肺的状态高于90%时会在冷藏时慢慢恢复，如果器官或其所在的容器从高处撞击地面，它会直接撞烂掉。

### Medical skill check: 40

A vital organ. The patient’s lung-related afflictions, such as [肺部损伤 Lung Damage](/afflictions/lung-damage), are directly linked to it.

The lungs may be removed or transplanted during an [器官移植手术 Organ Transplant Surgery](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery) done on the torso. Removal of the lungs causes immediate [呼吸停止 Respiratory Arrest](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest), 100% [肺部损伤 Lung Damage](/afflictions/lung-damage), and up to 20% [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage), inversely proportional to lung damage before removal. The condition of the organ is inversely proportional to lung damage before removal. The patient cannot gain and will be immediately relieved of [气胸 Pneumothorax](/afflictions/pneumothorax), and will gain [缺氧 Oxygen Low](/afflictions/oxygen-low) even if they are receiving [artificial ventilation](/items/surgery-table) or wearing an [自动心肺复苏器 Autopulse](/items/autopulse), while their lungs are removed.

If the lungs are transplanted back into the patient, their [肺部损伤 Lung Damage](/afflictions/lung-damage) and up to 20% [原版器官损伤](/afflictions/vanilla-organ-damage), both inversely proportional to the condition of the organ, will be treated, but their [呼吸停止 Respiratory Arrest](/afflictions/respiratory-arrest) will not be treated. The patient may gain [器官排斥 Organ Rejection](/afflictions/organ-rejection) if they receive the organ and their [免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) is higher than 10%, if the mechanic is toggled on in the mod config.

The condition of the lungs will decay unless placed in a [手提器官冷藏箱 Refrigerated Container](/items/refrigerated-container) or [器官冷藏集装箱 Refrigerated Crate](/items/refrigerated-crate). While refrigerated, the organ will slowly regain condition if it is above 90%, otherwise it will not change. If the organ or the container it is in hits the ground from a large height, it will be destroyed.
