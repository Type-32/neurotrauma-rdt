_又称自动CPR或自动脉冲_
医生的最好朋友。要使用自动心肺复苏仪，只需把它戴在你的潜水服插槽中。在佩戴和配备电池的情况下，自动脉冲可以治疗[心搏骤停](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest)，勉强减少[低血氧 Hypoxemia](/afflictions/hypoxemia) 和[心颤 Fibrillation](/afflictions/fibrillation) 并提高你的血压。但要注意不要过度使用它，因为它会使你的血压上升到[不需要的水平](/afflictions/hypertension) 。对躯干使用[扳手 Wrench](/items/wrench) 来从人身上强制移除。
A medic's best friend. To use the autopulse, simply wear it in your diving suit slot. While worn and equipped with batteries, the autopulse will cure [心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest) and slightly treat [缺氧 Oxygen Low](/afflictions/oxygen-low) and [心颤 Fibrillation](/afflictions/fibrillation), as well as increasing your blood pressure. Be wary to not overuse it, however, as it will increase your blood pressure into [unwanted levels](/afflictions/hypertension). It can be forcibly removed by using the [扳手 Wrench](/items/wrench)  as a treatment on the torso of a person wearing it.
