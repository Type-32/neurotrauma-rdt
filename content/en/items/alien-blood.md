### 医疗水平需求：40

用异星血浆代替普通[血袋](/items/blood-packs)是极其危险的，只能在紧急的情况下使用。
Using Alien Blood in place of regular blood is extremely dangerous and should only be used in dire situations.

\-20% [失血](/afflictions/blood-loss)
\+60% [精神病](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Psychosis)

\-20% [失血](/afflictions/blood-loss)
