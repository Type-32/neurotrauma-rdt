免疫力有助于对抗[伤口感染 Infected Wounds](/afflictions/infected-wounds) 并增强自然愈合。如果你的免疫力为6%或更低，你可以输不相容的血型而不触发[输血性休克 Hemotransfusion Shock](/afflictions/hemotransfusion-shock) 。如果你在配置菜单中打开了器官排斥，免疫力必须低于10%才能安全移植器官。

## **因以下原因减少：**

- [硫唑嘌呤 Azathioprine](/items/azathioprine)
- 对抗[伤口感染 Infected Wounds](/afflictions/infected-wounds)

## **有助于治愈：**

- [烧伤 Burns](/afflictions/burns)
- [开放性伤口 Open Wounds](/afflictions/open-wounds)



---

Immunity helps fight [伤口感染 Infected Wounds](/afflictions/infected-wounds) and increases natural healing. If your immunity is 6% or lower, you can use incompatible blood types without triggering [输血性休克 Hemotransfusion Shock](/afflictions/hemotransfusion-shock). Immunity must be below 10% to safely transplant organs if you have organ rejection turned on in the config menu.
