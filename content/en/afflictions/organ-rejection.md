此机制默认处于关闭状态。它可以在启用mod时在ESC菜单中的mod配置里切换。

器官排斥可能会在患者的 [免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) 高于10%时出现。当器官排斥出现时，移植的器官会立刻变为100%损伤，导致移植失败。

减少[免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity)  的主要手段为在手术前使用 [硫唑嘌呤 Azathioprine](/items/azathioprine) 。

在100% [免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity)  时发生器官排斥的概率为60% 。公式如下，其中1为100%：

This mechanic is off by default. It can be toggled in the mod’s config, found in the ESC menu while the mod is enabled.

Organ rejection may occur when a patient receives an organ transplant while their [免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) is higher than 10%. If organ rejection occurs, the organ that was transplanted will immediately gain 100% damage, making it useless.

Reducing [免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity) is primarily done using [硫唑嘌呤 Azathioprine](/items/azathioprine).

At 100% [免疫力 Immunity](/afflictions/immunity), the chance for organ rejection is 60%. The formula, where 1 is 100%, is as follows:
