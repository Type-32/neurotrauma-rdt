呼吸停止将迅速导致[缺氧](/afflictions/oxygen-low) ，使病人窒息，必须迅速处理。靠近[手术台](/items/surgery-table)或穿戴自动脉冲将防止呼吸停止产生的负面影响。
Respiratory arrest will quickly build up oxygen low and suffocate the victim, and must be dealt with swiftly. Being near a Surgery Table will prevent the negative effects of respiratory arrest from taking place.

---

## 由以下原因造成：

- [肺部损伤](/afflictions/lung-damage)(达到100%)
- [创伤性休克](/afflictions/traumatic-shock)(30%及以上)
- [神经创伤](/afflictions/neurotrauma) (100%及以上)
- [低血氧](/afflictions/hypoxemia)(70%及以上)
- [阿片类药物中毒](/afflictions/opiate-overdose) (50%及以上)
- [停滞袋](/items/stasis-bag)

## 导致：

- [缺氧](/afflictions/oxygen-low)
- [酸中毒](/afflictions/acidosis)

## 治疗措施

- 治疗病因
- 装有液氧罐的[急救呼吸气囊 Ambubag](/items/ambubag) 

## 急救

- 靠近[手术台](/items/surgery-table)
- [自动起搏器](/items/autopulse)
