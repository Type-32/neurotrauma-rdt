使用赛博义肢的一大缺点在于，其电子元件一旦接触水就会受损。

当赛博义肢受到伤害时，不同的伤害类型会转换为不同类型和数值的赛博肢体专属伤害，而非常规伤害类型。

设备损坏的强度随[结构消失 Material Loss](/afflictions/material-loss) 的强度的增加而增加，在[结构消失 Material Loss](/afflictions/material-loss) 达100%时最高可达3倍。

## **由以下原因造成：**

- 不穿潜水服入水
- [烧伤 Burns](/afflictions/burns) 有75%概率造成200%对应伤害的设备损坏
- 枪伤有85%概率造成75%对应伤害的设备损坏
- 咬伤有50%概率造成25%对应伤害的设备损坏
- 深层组织损伤有50%概率造成50%对应伤害的设备损坏
- 钝器伤有50%概率造成100%对应伤害的设备损坏
- [内伤 Internal Damage](/afflictions/internal-damage) 有75%的概率造成100%对应伤害的设备损坏
- [体内异物 Foreign Bodies](/afflictions/foreign-bodies) 有75%概率造成75%对应伤害的设备损坏

## 导致：

- 肢体偶尔会停工，频率随强度增加而增加。
- 肢体不可用（达到99%及以上）

## 治疗措施：

- [FPGA电路板 FPGA Circuit](/items/fpga-circuit)

---

The primary detriment of using cyberlimbs, as their electronics are damaged when exposed to water.

When taking damage to a cyberlimb, each type of damage has a chance to cause various types and amounts of cyberlimb specific damage instead of the normal damage type.

The amount of damaged electronics gained scales with the amount of [结构消失 Material Loss](/afflictions/material-loss) , up to a maximum of x3 at 100% material loss)

## Caused by:

- Being in water without a diving suit on
- 75% chance for [烧伤 Burns](/afflictions/burns)  to cause 200% of their damage as damaged electronics
- 85% chance for gunshot wounds to cause 75% of their damage as damaged electronics
- 50% chance for bite wounds to cause 25% of their damage as damaged electronics
- 50% chance for deep tissue damage to cause 50% of its damage as damaged electronics
- 50% chance for blunt force trauma to cause 100% of its damage as damaged electronics
- 75% chance for [内伤 Internal Damage](/afflictions/internal-damage)  to cause 100% of its damage as damaged electronics
- 75% chance for [体内异物 Foreign Bodies](/afflictions/foreign-bodies)  to cause 75% of its damage as damaged electronics

## Effects:

- Limb occassionally stops working temporarily, frequency scales with strength
- Limb becomes unusable (at 99% or more)

## Treatments:

- [FPGA电路板 FPGA Circuit](/items/fpga-circuit)
