心律不齐是指心脏不按正常速度跳动，可导致[心搏骤停](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest) 。
Irregular heartbeat is when the heart is not beating at its normal rate and can lead to [心搏骤停 Cardiac Arrest](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest).

---
##由以下原因造成：
- [心颤](/afflictions/fibrillation)

##导致：
- [心搏骤停](/afflictions/cardiac-arrest) (达到20%及以上)
- [低血压](/afflictions/hypotension)

##治疗措施：
- [手动除颤器](/items/manual-defibrillator)或[AED](/items/aed)
- CPR
- [自动心肺复苏仪](/items/autopulse)
- 治疗根本病因
