绷带是一种用途极其广泛的急救物品。建议每个船员在任何时候都至少带上一些绷带。绷带如果敷在[伤口](/afflictions/open-wounds)上久了会变脏，出血会使绷带比其他伤口更快变脏。绷带可以用[创伤剪](/items/trauma-shears)或[潜水刀](/items/diving-knife)取下

## 由以下原因造成：:

- [绷带](/items/bandages)和[可塑人造皮肤](/items/plastiseal)

## 效果:

- 稳定[脱臼](/afflictions/dislocations)和[骨折](/afflictions/fractures)，防止其造成[内伤](/afflictions/internal-damage)或[剧痛](/symptoms/intense-pain)。还可预防颅骨骨折引起的[神经创伤](/afflictions/neurotrauma)和肋骨骨折引起的[气胸](/afflictions/pneumothorax)。
- 消除脱臼的减速惩罚
- 每个包扎让你减速10%
- 加快[伤口](/afflictions/open-wounds)的自然恢复速度
- 防止[伤口感染](/afflictions/infected-wounds)

---

Bandages are an extremely versatile first aid item. It is advised that every crewmember keeps atleast some bandages on them at all times. Bandages will be turned dirty if they are applied on [开放性伤口 Open Wounds](/afflictions/open-wounds), with bleeding making a bandage dirty much faster than other wounds. Bandaged can be removed with [创伤剪 Trauma Shears](/items/trauma-shears) or [潜水刀 Diving Knife](/items/diving-knife).

## Caused by:

- [绷带 Bandages](/items/bandages) and [可塑人造皮肤 Plastiseal](/items/plastiseal)

## Effects:

- Prevents [脱臼 Dislocations](/afflictions/dislocations) and [骨折 Fractures](/afflictions/fractures) from causing [内伤 Internal Damage](/afflictions/internal-damage) or  [剧痛 Intense pain](/symptoms/intense-pain). Also prevents [气胸 Pneumothorax](/afflictions/pneumothorax) from rib fractures. Except for rib and neck fractures, dirty bandages also have this effect
- Removes slow down penalty and arm locking from [脱臼 Dislocations](/afflictions/dislocations). Dirty bandages also have this effect
- Slows you down by 10% for each bandage
- Accelerates natural healing of [开放性伤口 Open Wounds](/afflictions/open-wounds) and [烧伤 Burns](/afflictions/burns)
- Protects against [伤口感染 Infected Wounds](/afflictions/infected-wounds)
