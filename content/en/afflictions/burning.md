被火烧时的症状，患者会逐步[烧伤 Burns](/afflictions/burns) 。被烧到的人会尖叫（除非处于[镇痛](/afflictions/analgesia)状态）并引火上身。

## 由以下原因造成：

- 与火焰亲密接触
- [Incendium Grenade](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Incendium_Grenade)  （燃素手榴弹）

## 导致：

- [烧伤 Burns](/afflictions/burns)

## 治疗措施：

- 时间
- [Fire Extinguisher](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Fire_Extinguisher)  （灭火器）
- 进入水中
- 穿上[Diving Suit](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Diving_Suit)  （潜水服）或其他提供压力免疫的物品

---

Caused by fires, the patient will gain [烧伤 Burns](/afflictions/burns) over time. A character affected by burning will scream (unless they are [painless](/afflictions/analgesia)) and be visibly lit on fire.

---

## Causes:

- Standing in a fire
- [Incendium Grenade](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Incendium_Grenade)

## Effects:

- [烧伤 Burns](/afflictions/burns)

## Treatments:

- Time
- [Fire Extinguisher](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Fire_Extinguisher)
- Being inside water
- Wearing a [Diving Suit](https://barotraumagame.com/wiki/Diving_Suit) or other pressure-resistant items
