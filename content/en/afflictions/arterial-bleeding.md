动脉破裂极其危险，需要尽快进行紧急护理。在受伤的肢体上使用[止血带 Tourniquet](/items/tourniquet) 可以为你争取治疗时间，但不应该在头部使用。可能破裂的动脉包括头部的颈动脉、手臂的肱动脉和腿部的股动脉。躯干动脉破裂属于[主动脉破裂 Aortic Rupture](/afflictions/aortic-rupture) 。

游戏中包括：股动脉破裂，肱动脉破裂
Arterial ruptures are extremely dangerous and require emergency care as soon as possible. Applying a [止血带 Tourniquet](/items/tourniquet) to the afflicted limb can buy you time to treat it, but this should not be done on the head. The arteries which may rupture include the carotid artery on the head, brachial arteries of the arms, and femoral arteries of the legs. Rupture of the torso artery is an [主动脉破裂 Aortic Rupture](/afflictions/aortic-rupture).
