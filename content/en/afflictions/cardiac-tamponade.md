在涉及潜艇外的战斗任务中，如废弃的前哨站或沉船，心脏压塞是很常见的。它的负面效果可以用[气胸针 Needle](/items/needle) 暂时抵消以等待彻底治疗。
Cardiac tamponade is common during missions involving combat outside the submarine, such as abandoned outposts or wrecks. Its negative effects can be temporarily negated with a [气胸针 Needle](/items/needle) until it is surgically treated.

---

## 由以下原因造成：

- 躯干上出现[开放性伤口 Open Wounds](/afflictions/open-wounds)

## 导致：

- [低血压](/afflictions/hypotension)（根据强度增长，到达100%时血压减半）
- [虚弱](/symptoms/weakness) (达到30%及以上)
- [咳嗽](/symptoms/cough) (达到20%及以上)
- [呼吸困难](/symptoms/shortness-of-breath) (达到10%及以上)

## 治疗措施

- 在手术中使用[引流管 Drainage](/items/drainage) （在使用[牵开器 Retractors](/items/retractors) 后）

## 急救

- [气胸针 Needle](/items/needle)
- [生理盐水 Saline](/items/saline)
- [林格氏液 Ringer's Solution](/items/ringer-s-solution)
