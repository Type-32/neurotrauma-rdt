[截肢手术](/procedures/amputation-surgery)的第五步。在附近没有[手术台](/items/surgery-table)的情况下，每一个锯开的骨头会导致每秒1.5%的[创伤性休克](/afflictions/traumatic-shock)。
The fifth step in [截肢手术 Amputation Surgery](/procedures/amputation-surgery). Having sawed bones without a surgery table nearby will cause 1.5% [创伤性休克 Traumatic Shock](/afflictions/traumatic-shock) per second for each instance of sawed bones.
