动脉夹闭是在[动脉出血 Arterial Bleeding](/afflictions/arterial-bleeding) 的肢体(除头部)上使用[止血带 Tourniquet](/items/tourniquet) 后的效果。在止血的肢体上完成[开始&结束手术 Open & Close Surgery](/procedures/open-close-surgery) 流程可以移除。

## 由以下原因造成：

- [止血带 Tourniquet](/items/tourniquet)

## 导致：

- 防止[动脉出血 Arterial Bleeding](/afflictions/arterial-bleeding)  造成[失血 Blood Loss](/afflictions/blood-loss)
- 在一段时间后造成[坏疽 Gangrene](/afflictions/gangrene)

---

Clamped bleeding is caused by using a [止血带 Tourniquet](/items/tourniquet) on an extremity (except the head) with an [动脉出血 Arterial Bleeding](/afflictions/arterial-bleeding). It is removed upon completing an [开始&结束手术 Open & Close Surgery](/procedures/open-close-surgery) on the affected limb.

---

## Caused by:

- [止血带 Tourniquet](/items/tourniquet)

## Effects:

- Prevents [动脉出血 Arterial Bleeding](/afflictions/arterial-bleeding) from causing [失血 Blood Loss](/afflictions/blood-loss)
- Causes [坏疽 Gangrene](/afflictions/gangrene) after a period of time
