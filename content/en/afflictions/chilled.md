凝胶冰袋的效果。大大增加了钝器伤的自然恢复速度。

## **由以下原因造成：**

- [凝胶冰袋 Gel Ice Pack](/items/gel-ice-pack)

## **导致：**

- 每个冰敷的肢体减少5%移动速度
- 极大加速[钝器伤](/afflictions/internal-damage)的恢复速度，除非其强度高于100
- 让[伤口感染 Infected Wounds](/afflictions/infected-wounds) 的状况更快恶化
- 每秒减0.2[内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding)

---

The affliction given by the gel ice pack when used. Greatly increases natural regeneration of blunt force trauma.

## Caused by:

- [凝胶冰袋 Gel Ice Pack](/items/gel-ice-pack)

## Effects:

- Slows you down by 5% for each iced limb
- Increases natural healing of [Blunt Force Trauma](/afflictions/internal-damage), unless if it is over 100 affliction strength
- Makes [伤口感染 Infected Wounds](/afflictions/infected-wounds) worsen faster
- -0.2% [内出血 Internal Bleeding](/afflictions/internal-bleeding) per second
