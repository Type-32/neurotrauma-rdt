大多数外科手术的第四步。在附近没有手术台的情况下，，每一处皮肤牵开会导致每秒2%的[创伤性休克](/afflictions/traumatic-shock)。大多数外科手术，如[骨合成手术](/procedures/osteosynthetic-surgery)或[器官移植手术](/procedures/organ-transplant-surgery)，都源于这一步骤。
The fourth step in most surgical procedures. Having retracted skin without a surgery table nearby will cause 2% https://trello.com/c/c3VraEEu/65-traumatic-shock per second for each instance of retracted skin. Most surgical procedures, such as https://trello.com/c/PAWSCJGJ/77-osteosynthetic-surgery or https://trello.com/c/LwhWr6ge/96-organ-transplant-surgery stem off from this step.
