失去意识通常意味着发生了非常严重的问题，医生需要尽快对你进行治疗。无意识会掩盖精神症状（被这种苦痛所隐藏的卡片会被贴上 "精神症状 "的标签），也会起到[手术台](/items/surgery-table)的作用，也就是说，只要病人无意识，你就可以在没有手术台或[镇痛](/afflictions/analgesia)的情况下安全地进行手术。
Unconsciousness usually means that something very wrong has happened, and a medic will need to treat you as soon as possible. Unconsciousness will hide mental symptoms such as [剧痛 Intense pain](/symptoms/intense-pain) or [视野模糊 Blurred Vision](/symptoms/blurred-vision), and will also function as a surgery table for surgery, meaning that you can safely do surgery without a table or [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia) as long as your patient is unconscious.
