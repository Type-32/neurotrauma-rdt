虚弱是一种症状，这意味着它是非致命的，不会引起任何其他的苦痛。虚弱会使你造成的近战伤害减少80%。
Weakness is a symptom, which means that it isn't fatal and doesn't cause any other afflictions. Weakness will decrease melee damage you deal by 80%.

---

- [心脏压塞](/afflictions/cardiac-tamponade) (30%及以上)
- [失血](/afflictions/blood-loss) (60%及以上)
- [酸中毒](/afflictions/acidosis) (35%及以上)
