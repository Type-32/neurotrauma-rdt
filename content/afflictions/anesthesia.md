麻醉是一种防止手术中[创伤性休克](/afflictions/traumatic-shock)的效果，与[镇痛](/afflictions/analgesia)不同，不需要[手术台](/items/surgery-table)就能进行安全手术。
麻醉会在达到100%的强度时会立即消失。麻醉也会使你的角色减速50%。

Anesthesia is an affliction that prevents traumatic shock during surgery, and unlike [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia), doesn't require a table to perform safe surgery.
Anesthesia will immediately wear off upon reaching 100% strength. Anesthesia will also slow your character down by 50%.

- [异丙酚](/items/propofol)

- 减少50%移动速度
