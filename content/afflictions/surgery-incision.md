**所有**外科手术的第二步。在没有手术钳止血的情况下，每个切口每秒会造成0.7%的[失血](/afflictions/blood-loss)，而在没有[镇痛]( "")效果或其他镇静方法（[失去意识](/afflictions/unconsciousness)，[麻醉](/afflictions/anesthesia)）的情况下，每个切口每秒会造成额外2%的[创伤性休克](/afflictions/traumatic-shock)。在有镇痛的情况下即使没有[手术台](/items/surgery-table)，也不会造成创伤性休克，因此这种情况下可以安心的实行放血疗法。
The second step in **all** surgical procedures. Having an unclamped surgical incision will cause 0.7% [失血 Blood Loss](/afflictions/blood-loss) per second for each incision, and having a surgical incision without [镇痛 Analgesia](/afflictions/analgesia) or another sedation method ([失去意识 Unconsciousness](/afflictions/unconsciousness), [麻醉 Anesthesia](/afflictions/anesthesia)) will cause 2% [创伤性休克 Traumatic Shock](/afflictions/traumatic-shock) per second for each incision. This affliction will not cause traumatic shock if done without a table, therefore making it save to bloodlet without one.
