## **由以下原因造成：**

- 在有螺丝松动时移动（达到30%及更高，越动越松）
- 撕裂伤有75%概率造成25%对应伤害的螺丝松动
- 深层组织损伤有80%概率造成100%对应伤害的螺丝松动
- 钝器伤有50%概率造成50%对应伤害的螺丝松动
- [内伤 Internal Damage](/afflictions/internal-damage) 有75%的概率造成100%对应伤害的螺丝松动
- 咬伤有50%概率造成50%对应伤害的螺丝松动
- [体内异物 Foreign Bodies](/afflictions/foreign-bodies) 有75%概率造成75%对应伤害的螺丝松动

## 导致：

- 螺丝松动和[结构消失 Material Loss](/afflictions/material-loss) （达到30%及更高）

## 治疗措施：

- [螺丝刀 Screwdriver](/items/screwdriver)

---

## Caused by:

- Loose screws while moving (30% or more)
- 75% chance for lacerations to cause 25% of their damage as loose screws
- 80% chance for deep tissue damage to cause 100% of its damage as loose screws
- 50% chance for blunt force trauma to cause 50% of its damage as loose screws
- 75% chance for [内伤 Internal Damage](/afflictions/internal-damage)  to cause 100% of its damage as loose screws
- 50% chance for bite wounds to cause 50% of their damage as loose screws
- 75% chance for [体内异物 Foreign Bodies](/afflictions/foreign-bodies)  to cause 75% of its damage as loose screws

## Effects:

- Loose screws and [结构消失 Material Loss](/afflictions/material-loss)  if moving (at 30% or more)

## Treatments:

- [螺丝刀 Screwdriver](/items/screwdriver)
