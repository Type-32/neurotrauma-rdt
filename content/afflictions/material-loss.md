当赛博义肢受到伤害时，不同的伤害类型会转换为不同类型和数值的赛博肢体专属伤害，而非常规伤害类型。

结构消失的强度随[螺丝松动 Loose Screws](/afflictions/loose-screws)  的强度的增加而增加，在[螺丝松动 Loose Screws](/afflictions/loose-screws) 达100%时最高可达3倍。

## **由以下原因造成：**

- 在有[螺丝松动 Loose Screws](/afflictions/loose-screws)  时移动（达到30%及更高，越动越松）
- 撕裂伤有75%概率造成25%对应伤害的结构消失
- 枪伤有80%概率造成80%对应伤害的结构消失
- 咬伤有75%概率造成60%对应伤害的结构消失
- 深层组织损伤有100%概率造成100%对应伤害的结构消失
- [体内异物 Foreign Bodies](/afflictions/foreign-bodies) 有80%概率造成50%对应伤害的结构消失

## 导致：

- 肢体损毁并造成[创伤性截肢 Traumatic Amputation](/afflictions/traumatic-amputation) （达到99%及更高）

## 治疗措施：

- [钢筋 Steel Bar](/items/steel-bar)

---

When taking damage to a cyberlimb, each type of damage has a chance to cause various types and amounts of cyberlimb specific damage instead of the normal damage type.

The amount of material loss gained scales with the amount of [螺丝松动 Loose Screws](/afflictions/loose-screws) , to a maximum of x3 at 100% loose screws)

## Caused by:

- [螺丝松动 Loose Screws](/afflictions/loose-screws)  while moving (30% or more)
- 75% chance for lacerations to cause 25% of their damage as material loss
- 80% chance for gunshot wounds to cause 80% of their damage as material loss
- 75% chance for bite wounds to cause 60% of their damage as material loss
- 100% chance for deep tissue injury to cause 100% of its damage as material loss
- 80% chance for foreign bodies to cause 50% of their damage as material loss

## Effects:

- Loss of cyberlimb and [创伤性截肢 Traumatic Amputation](/afflictions/traumatic-amputation)  (at 99% or more)

## Treatments:

- [钢筋 Steel Bar](/items/steel-bar)
