心颤是一个三个步骤的过程。起初，它将以心率加快开始，并将缓慢增加。当达到100%的心率增加时，它将变成心律不齐，这也会缓慢增加，并在达到100%时变成心搏骤停。此外，心律不齐有可能立即转变为心脏骤停，其概率取决于你的心律不齐的程度。在[肾上腺素](/items/adrenaline)的作用下，心颤的增速会减半。在硝普钠或硝化甘油的作用下，由低血压引起的心颤会减少。
Fibrillation is a 3-step process. At first, it will begin as increased heartrate, which will increase slowly. Upon reaching 100% increased heartrate, it will become irregular heartbeat, which will also increase, and will become cardiac arrest upon hitting 100%. Additionally, there is a chance for irregular heartbeat to immediately skip to cardiac arrest, which scales depending on how much irregular heartrate you have. Fibrillation gain is halved when under the effects of Adrenaline, and fibrillation caused by hypotension is reduced when under the effects of Sodium Nitroprusside or Nitroglycerin.
